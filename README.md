# 05 Into the Wind

**▶ Play it:** https://hanji-xu.github.io/into-the-wind/

A cozy little pet simulation, but when it's windy. A fox, a cat, a husky, a bison or a shaggy dog lies on a rug. Drag or click anywhere around it to move the wind. After a moment it turns its head into the wind, and then shuffles its body round to follow. Every hair moves on its own: wind from behind ruffles the coat and blows it into the animal's eyes, and wind from the front combs it flat.

Stroke the fur to pet it. The fur parts under your hand, its eyes close, and hearts float up. Press space for a gust.

Part of **Art Insights**, a series of interactive pieces by Hanji Xu. Open `index.html` in a current browser; it is a single self-contained file with no network access or libraries. See also [01 Stubble Field](https://github.com/Hanji-Xu/stubble-vector-field).
